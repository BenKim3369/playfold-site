Website privacy

Updated 27 November 2026.

We count visits to the English game page and clicks on its App Store buttons to understand how people move from the page to the store. Counts are grouped by day, button, and a small set of sources such as an Instagram ad or a direct visit. They do not tell us whether anyone bought or installed the game.

This measurement uses no cookies, browser storage, advertising identifiers, or persistent visitor profiles. A random reference lasts for the current page only and prevents repeated clicks from being counted twice. The reference is removed from our records after about two days. Only daily totals remain, for about one year.

Our measurement records do not contain names, email addresses, IP addresses, browser details, full referring addresses, or URL query strings. Only recognized campaign labels are counted; other query values are discarded. Cloudflare processes the connection needed to receive these counts, and GitHub Pages hosts the website; their own service and security practices apply.

Following an App Store link opens Apple’s service, which has its own privacy practices. Read the game privacy policy for information about the app.
